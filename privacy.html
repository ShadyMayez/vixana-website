Privacy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Your data stays on your computer

The Vixana desktop app and the vixana command line train models on the computer they are installed on. The tables you give Vixana, the models it trains and the predictions it makes are stored only in folders on that computer. Vixana does not send them anywhere.

Signing in with Google

The desktop app asks you to sign in with your Google account. Google then gives Vixana your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID, and nothing else: Vixana asks only for the openid, email and profile permissions. Vixana keeps these details on your computer, in its local sign-in database, so it knows who is using the app. It does not keep your Google password or any Google access token, and it cannot read your mail, files or anything else in your Google account.

If someone runs a shared Vixana server and you sign in there, that server stores the same details and a session, and its operator is responsible for them.

Your Vixana account

When you sign in on this website, or link the vixana command line to your account with vixana login, the Vixana accounts service keeps your Google account ID, name, email address and picture, the time you signed up and last signed in, and a list of the command lines you linked (the computer's name and when). It keeps the command line's sign-in tokens only as one-way hashes. This service runs on Supabase (servers in Frankfurt, Germany), which processes it as described in the Supabase Privacy Policy. It does not receive your data, models or predictions; they stay on your computer.

This website keeps your sign-in in your browser's local storage (not a cookie) until you sign out. To delete your account, open an issue at the address below or email the owner, and it will be removed.

Network use

  • On first start, the app and the install scripts download Vixana's Python packages from PyPI and PyTorch's package index, and the command-line installer downloads Vixana from GitHub.
  • Signing in contacts Google to confirm who you are, and the Vixana accounts service when you use a Vixana account.
  • Vixana has no analytics, tracking or advertising, and it sets no cookies on this website.

This website

This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visits (for example IP addresses) as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site itself collects nothing.

Removing your data

Uninstall the app and delete its folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Vixana on Windows, ~/.local/share/vixana on Linux and macOS). To remove Vixana's access in Google, open Third-party connections in your Google account.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/ShadyMayez/vixana-website.
